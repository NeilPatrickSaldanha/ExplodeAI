## Script

**[0:00 Hook. Screen: Lesson 03 diagram, drag the Explode slider from 0 to 100%]**

This is a sentence turning into numbers inside an AI model. I'm pulling the machine apart so you can see every piece. This is ExplodeAI, and this video is a quick tour of what it is and how it works.

**[0:20 Who and why. Screen: face or the home page]**

I'm Neil. I like building things that connect AI to real systems, and I learn much better from seeing something happen than from reading paragraphs about it. So I built a place where AI engineering is something you can pull apart, poke, break on purpose and put back together.

One honest note: I built this with Claude Code. I set the direction, the lessons and the quality bar, and I'm making sure I understand everything I show you. I'm not hiding that.

**[1:00 The home page and sidebar. Screen: home page, then the lesson list]**

There are five lessons so far: RAG, tokenization, embeddings, attention, and tool-using agents. They're in the sidebar, and a tick appears when you've explored every stage of one. Each lesson takes about 25 to 30 minutes.

**[1:20 The words switch. Screen: toggle Plain words / Technical names at the top]**

Up here is a switch. I made this for people who haven't learned any of this yet. Plain words is the default: everyday names for every part. Switch to Technical names and you get the standard terms. The facts underneath are the same.

**[1:45 The big picture. Screen: open the big picture bar]**

Every lesson starts with a "big picture": the whole thing in a few sentences, one analogy for every part, and one running example that follows through all the stages.

**[2:05 The diagram. Screen: diagram, slider, buttons along the bottom]**

This is the machine. Each block is one stage. The slider pulls it apart. Along the bottom: Flow shows what moves from stage to stage, Data shows the real values from your last run, Isolate lets you look at one part alone, 3D and 2D, and Calm turns the motion down. [OPTIONAL: Move lets you slide the diagram around.]

**[2:40 A stage. Screen: click "Meaning reader", scroll the stage panel]**

Click a part and you get its panel. What goes in. What comes out. A story in plain words, then what happens to our running example, with real numbers. An analogy, and it also tells you where the analogy stops being true. "Words you will hear" introduces the technical terms one at a time. And a question to check yourself before you reveal the answer.

**[3:20 What breaks. Screen: "What breaks if removed?" then source-backed facts]**

My favourite part: "What breaks if removed". I take that stage out and the machine really re-runs without it, so you see what it was for. Below that are the facts with links to their sources.

**[3:50 Five modes. Screen: tabs Explore, Tour, Experiment, Break & fix, Rebuild]**

There are five ways in. Explore is clicking around. Tour walks the stages one by one. Experiment lets you change settings on a real run, and it tells you what to try and what to notice. Break & fix hands you something broken and asks you to find out why. Rebuild asks you to put the stages back in an order that actually works.

**[4:40 The tutor. Screen: Ask ExplodeAI button]**

And there's an Ask ExplodeAI helper. It's meant to answer from the lessons, and to say so when something isn't covered.

**[4:55 Honesty. Screen: the Real execution / Simulation badges, the Verified badge]**

Everything is labelled as real, simulated or just a picture. The data is made up, so nothing here claims anything about the world. Each fact links to its source and went through a three-step check. I'm not claiming it's perfect. That's why the sources are there.

**[5:20 Series plan. Screen: lesson sidebar]**

From the next video on, each one takes a single topic and goes deep: RAG, tokenization, embeddings, attention, and agents. If you have a question or something looks wrong, tell me.

**[5:40 Close]**

[If public:] You can try it at the link. Next up: how an AI looks something up before it answers.
